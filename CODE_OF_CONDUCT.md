# Code of Conduct

English | [中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh.md)

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community. Participants must not discriminate against or harass others based on personal characteristics, identity, background, or technical experience.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainers through the security contact or GitHub reporting tools. Maintainers may remove comments, restrict participation, or close contributions when necessary to protect the community.

## Attribution

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant community standard.
